Privacy policy

How we collect, store and protect donor data, including PAN and contact details.

Read summary

We collect your name, contact details and PAN only to process donations, issue 80G receipts and send updates you opt into. We never sell or share your data with third parties for marketing. Terms of service

Terms for using this website, donor accounts and recurring donations.

Read summary

Donations are voluntary contributions to SikhAid Charitable Trust. Funds raised for a campaign are used for that response, with any surplus applied to its next phase. Monthly donations continue until you pause or cancel them from your account.

Refund & cancellation

How to request a refund or cancel a monthly gift.

Read summary

If you donated by mistake, email help@sikhaid.ngo within 7 days with your receipt number. Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 7–10 working days. Monthly gifts can be cancelled anytime before the next debit.
